Create a design canvas for FantasyGuru — the v1 Android app.

Read docs/design-brief.md first and follow it exactly. It contains the product
description, the aesthetic thesis, the full colour/type/spacing system, the
artboard list, and the real content for every screen. Do not substitute
placeholder content: every player, league, score and timestamp in the brief is
specified and internally consistent (Week 6, Sunday 18 October 2026, 10:14 AM,
2h 46m to kickoff, four real bye teams). Use it verbatim.

Artboards: 412 x 915 dp Android phone frames, drawn edge-to-edge with a 24dp
status-bar inset at top and a 24dp gesture-nav inset at bottom. Ten artboards in
three rows:

  Row 1 - Onboarding   01 Cold open  |  02 Finding your leagues
  Row 2 - Core loop    03 Home (calm)  |  04 Home (alarm)  |  05 League detail  |
                       06 Players  |  07 Live
  Row 3 - System       08 Notification  |  09 Permission pre-prompt  |
                       10 Empty & error

Design the dark theme. It is the primary and the one the app ships in.

The single most important constraint, from section 2 of the brief: the interface
is monochrome slate, and the only saturated colour anywhere on screen is status —
red, amber, green. The interactive accent is a desaturated steel blue that reads
as "tappable", never as "important". No colour for branding, decoration,
emphasis, illustration, or category coding. If an element needs to stand out, use
size, weight, or space instead.

Start with artboard 03, Home in its calm state, and get it right before moving
on. It is the screen users see most weeks and the hardest to make feel resolved
rather than empty. Artboard 04 is the screen the whole product exists for — the
verdict must be readable at arm's length, at a glance, without reading a word.

Two things to resist: this should not look like a stock Material 3 app, and it
should not look like a dashboard. There are no stat tiles, no charts, and no
gamification anywhere in this product.
